OUR STORY

Every community begins with people who care.

Reddys Youth Association is a platform created to bring together the youth of Guvvalegi and create a stronger sense of community, responsibility and belonging.


From community service and youth activities to cultural celebrations and opportunities for young leaders, RYA aims to create a space where people can connect, contribute and grow together.

Our Vision

To build a united and empowered community where young people take initiative, support one another and contribute meaningfully to the future of Guvvalegi.

OUR ROOTS

Guvvalegi is more than a place.

It is our memories, our people, our traditions and the stories passed from one generation to another.


RYA aims to help preserve that connection while giving the next generation a platform to participate and shape the future.
